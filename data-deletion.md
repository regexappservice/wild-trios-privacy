# Wild Trios — Data deletion request

**Last updated:** September 24, 2026

Wild Trios does not create a user account or store personal profile data on our servers. Gameplay preferences (for example sound, Scout, selected animal, and difficulty) are stored only on your device.

## How to delete data on your device

1. Uninstall Wild Trios from your device. That removes local app data we store on the device.
2. Optionally reset your device advertising identifier in system settings (this affects personalized ads across apps).

## Advertising / Google data

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Data handled by Google for advertising is subject to Google’s policies. You can manage ad personalization choices through in-app privacy / ad options (where shown) and through your Google account / device advertising settings.

## Request help from us

If you need help with a deletion-related request, contact the developer using the support email listed on the Wild Trios Google Play or App Store listing. We do not hold a separate online account for you; we will confirm what (if anything) remains and how to clear it.
