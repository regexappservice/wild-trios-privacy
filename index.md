# Wild Trios — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** July 24, 2026

Wild Trios (“the App”) is a casual single-player game. This policy explains what information the App and its advertising partners handle.

The App does not require an account and does not intentionally ask you for personal information such as your name, email address, or precise location.

Gameplay preferences (for example sound effects, Scout mode, selected animal, and difficulty) are stored only on your device so the App can remember your choices between sessions.

The App shows advertisements through Google AdMob (and may use related Google advertising services). Ads may appear as a banner on the home / match setup screen, between matches (interstitials), or when you choose to watch a rewarded ad. If you purchase Wild Trios Plus (or an equivalent Remove Ads product), banners and interstitials are disabled; optional rewarded ads may still be offered where the product allows.

Where allowed by law and by your choices, ads may be personalized using information collected by Google and its partners—for example advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID or IDFA), IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and app information, and data about how you interact with ads. That information is used to deliver, measure, and improve ads.

In regions that require consent (for example the EEA and UK), and where Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) applies, the App will ask for your advertising consent before requesting personalized ads. If you decline personalized ads, or if consent is not granted, the App may still show non-personalized ads when permitted.

On iOS, Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt may also ask permission before tracking across apps and websites for advertising. Denying ATT does not block you from playing; it may limit personalized advertising.

You can change advertising privacy choices where the App offers a privacy / ad options entry (via UMP), and through your device settings (for example limiting ad tracking or resetting your advertising identifier). Google’s own policies also apply to AdMob; see Google’s advertising and privacy materials for partner disclosures.

If you install the App from the Apple App Store or Google Play, those platforms may collect information under their own privacy policies. We do not control that collection. In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google; we do not receive your full payment card details.

We may update this policy when advertising, analytics, or purchases change. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy. The “Last updated” date above shows when this version took effect.

## Data deletion

Wild Trios does not create a user account or keep personal profile data on our servers. To remove local App data, uninstall the App. For advertising identifiers and Google ad data, use your device / Google advertising settings. For help with a deletion request, see [Data deletion](data-deletion.md) or contact the developer using the support channel listed on the App’s store listing.

If you have questions about this policy, contact the developer using the support channel listed on the App’s store listing.
